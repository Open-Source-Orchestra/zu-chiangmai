Licenses & care

Original module code

GPL-3.0-or-later. Read the license. Corresponding generator and browser source are included under ./source/.

Original words & abstract lantern/ridge art

CC0 1.0 Universal; see the dedication. This applies only to new module wording and original abstract art.

Third-party recordings, words & images

Speakers, performers, photographers and other third parties retain their rights. The broad upstream CC0 wording is not treated as relicensing their voices, performances, likenesses or works. The upstream declaration is CC0-1.0 · no rights reserved · fork it, but it does not clear third-party rights. Source recordings, machine transcripts, titles, credits, performances and posters retain their existing source rights. No blanket CC0 claim is made for them.

Reuse is limited to records and thumbnails already published in the public 4SeasPIT content pool. Public per-person permission receipts are not itemized; this prototype does not assert new consent or add new people/media.

Consent & takedown

Public consent protocol ↗ · Takedown first ↗

Apply a local ID denylist before render/search; remove entry, poster, transcript and cached search hits immediately, and never let a later sync re-add it. External Drive/R2/GitHub copies require their own upstream removal handling.
